African Wild Dog

The wild dog is one of the world’s most endangered mammals. The largest populations remain in southern Africa and the southern part of East Africa. The population is about 1,409 and major threats to the survival of wild dogs include accidental and targeted killings by humans, viral diseases like rabies and distemper, habitat loss, and competition with larger predators like lions. One way to help is supporting the creation of protected areas for African wild dogs.

Emperor Penguin

The emperor is the largest living penguin species standing around 115 centimeters tall. Once they have found a partner, they work together to keep their young fed and safe. Most penguins feed on krill, fish, and squid, caught while swimming underwater. The emperor penguin is the only species to breed during the harsh Antarctic winter, on sea ice. They are endangered due to their sea ice habitat shrinking and is likely to continue to do so over the next several decades. One way to help is to reduce your use of emissions that increase climate change.

European Mink

The European mink is one of the most threatened mammals in Europe. Once widespread across Europe, the European mink now occupies less than 20% of its former range and is currently restricted to a handful of isolated populations in northern Spain/southwestern France, the Danube delta in Romania and Ukraine, and parts of Russia. A big threat to European mink is competition with the invasive non-native American mink, following decades of sustained habitat loss and over-hunting. One way you can help is by helping reduce American minks in the habitats of European minks.

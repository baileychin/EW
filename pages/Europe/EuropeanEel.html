European Eel

The European eel is a type of eel that resides mostly in the United Kingdom.They have declined by around 90% since the 1980s and is one of only a few UK species considered to be ‘critically endangered’. It is disappearing faster from our rivers than any other fish despite being considered one of the toughest and most resilient of fish species.The eel has many threats which reduce spawning escapement, cause poorer ocean survival, blocked river access and local mortality.

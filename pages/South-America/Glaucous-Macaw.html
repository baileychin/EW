Glaucous Macaw

The glaucous macaw is a critically endangered or possibly extinct species of large, blue and grey South American macaw.The bird's disappearance probably is linked to trapping of live adults for the wild bird trade and the wholesale felling of the yatay palm, Butia yatay, the nuts of which appear to have constituted its main food. One way to help is to support efforts in conserving their environment.

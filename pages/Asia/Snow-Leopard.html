Snow Leopard

Snow leopards play a key role as a top predator, an indicator of the health of their high-altitude habitat, and, increasingly, an important indicator of the impacts of climate change on mountain environments.There us about 4,000–6,500. One of the things impacting them is the illegal wildlife trade. Snow leopards are poached for the illegal trade. Things we do to prevent this illegal trade is by working with the government, enforcement agencies, local communities, and conservation organizations to stop wildlife criminal networks and the illegal trade of snow leopard fur, bones, and other body parts.

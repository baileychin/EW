Southern Cassowary

The southern cassowary is one of three species of cassowary. Impressive and intimidating-looking, these flightless birds are said to resemble dinosaurs. They are native to tropical rainforests of Australia and New Guinea. Many threats endanger the Southern Cassowary, mostly because of increasing human presence in its habitat. The main causes of cassowary deaths are habitat loss, vehicle strikes, dog attacks and disease. Fewer than 1,500 of these birds survive in Australia. One way to help is by consuming less.
